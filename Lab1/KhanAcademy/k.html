Recipe 1: Greek Salad

  • Time: 15 min
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: easy

Ingredients:

Ingredient Quantity
Tomatoes 3 pieces
Cucumbers 2 pieces
Red onion 1 piece
Feta cheese 200 g
Olives 100 g
Olive oil 3 tbsp

Instructions:

  1. Cut tomatoes and cucumbers into cubes
  2. Slice onion into thin rings
  3. Cut cheese into cubes
  4. Mix all ingredients in a bowl
  5. Add olives
  6. Add olive oil and mix well

Recipe 2: Cheese Omelette

  • Time: 10 min
  • Servings: 2
  • Difficulty: easy

Ingredients:

Ingredient Quantity
Eggs 4 pieces
Milk 50 ml
Hard cheese 100 g
Salt to taste
Butter 1 tbsp

Instructions:

  1. Beat eggs with milk and salt
  2. Grate the cheese
  3. Melt butter in a pan
  4. Pour egg mixture into the pan
  5. Sprinkle with cheese
  6. Cook on medium heat for 5-7 minutes

Source: EdimDoma.ru

Recipe 3: Oatmeal Cookies

  • Time: 30 min
  • Servings: 20 cookies
  • Difficulty: medium

Ingredients:

Ingredient Quantity
Oatmeal 200 g
Flour 150 g
Sugar 100 g
Butter 150 g
Egg 1 piece
Baking powder 1 tsp

Instructions:

  1. Soften butter at room temperature
  2. Mix butter with sugar
  3. Add egg and mix well
  4. Add flour, oatmeal and baking powder
  5. Knead the dough
  6. Form cookies and bake 15-20 minutes at 180°C

Source: Povarenok.ru
